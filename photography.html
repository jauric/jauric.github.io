When I'm on the road, I like to look for the little details that set a place apart and sometimes tell their own story about the local culture and history. I don't use special equipment or editing, my phone usually does the job. One day I should get better gear I guess... Some of the shots I took over the years nevertheless turned out to be quite interesting and I decided to put them online for everyone to enjoy. A lot of content is still catching digital dust and will be uploaded over time.
